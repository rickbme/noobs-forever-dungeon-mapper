# Credits

## OpenMap

Noobs Forever Dungeon Mapper is modelled on [OpenMap](https://github.com/openmap-world/openmap), an open source
world map addon for WoW Forever. OpenMap's code, data tables and tools are MIT licensed
(Copyright (c) 2026 OpenMap contributors), and we reuse and adapt them under that licence. The MIT
notice is kept below as the licence requires.

Where a file is adapted from OpenMap, its first lines say so. So far:

| File | Adapted from |
| --- | --- |
| `tools/test/lint.py` | `tools/offline-test/lint.py` |
| `tools/casc/casc.py` | `tools/casc/casc.py` |
| `tools/map-dungeons/minimap.py`, `minimap.json` | `tools/map-dungeons/minimap.py`, `minimap.json` (floors for Ruins of Lordaeron and The Hall of Thanes are OpenMap's; City of Dalaran is ours) |
| `tools/map-dungeons/drawn.py` | `tools/map-dungeons/drawn.py` (turns Reyes' maps into tiles) |
| `tools/map-world/places.lua` | `OpenMap/Data/Forever/Places.lua` (dungeon entrances, copied as is) |
| `NoobsForeverDungeonMapper/Data/DungeonBosses.lua` | `OpenMap/Data/Forever/DungeonBosses.lua` (boss pin positions, copied as is) |
| `NoobsForeverDungeonMapper/Data/DungeonMinimap.lua` | generated by the ported minimap.py, same format as OpenMap's |

## Santiago Reyes

The dungeon maps in `art/drawn/dungeons/` (City of Dalaran and The Underbelly, The Drowned City, Blackmaw
Hold, Excavation Site: Wetlands, Ruins of Lordaeron, Shaper's Terrace, Krol'dok Stronghold, The Hall of
Thanes) are by **Santiago Reyes**, from his project
[Atlas de Azeroth: Forever](https://sreyesmaps.com/post/proyecto-atlas-de-azeroth-forever). They are his
art, not covered by this repo's MIT licence, and not Blizzard's official maps.

His announcement of 25 September 2026 says addon authors may use the maps if the project is non-commercial
and doesn't go against Blizzard's EULA, the watermarks and credits stay unchanged, and he is told how they
are used. We follow that: the watermarks are untouched, the map window shows "Map by Santiago Reyes", and
we have written to ask and thank him (also see PLAN.md). If he asks us to remove or change anything, we do.

## Not covered by OpenMap's MIT licence

OpenMap says its licence excludes these, so we do not copy them without the owner's permission:

- Textures made from Blizzard's art. We cut some from the game install ourselves (`Media/NOTICE.md`).
- `art/edits/`, the hand-edited copies of Blizzard's art.
- `art/chart/` (sea chart) and `art/gaps/` (painted gaps between zones), also by Reyes. His announcement
  covers dungeon maps; we have not asked about these yet, so they are not included.
- OpenMap's icon (drawn by zloyeb, used with permission).
